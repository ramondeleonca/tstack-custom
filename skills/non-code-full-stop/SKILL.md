# Non-code Full Stop

Read [non-code-full-send](../non-code-full-send/SKILL.md) and follow its
steps, proof, review, and gate. A "full stop" request authorizes that work.
The overrides below take precedence.

## Never do the final step

Do not send, post, share, submit, delete, or overwrite anything outside your
own drafts, even if the request names recipients or targets. Save drafts
where the user will find them: a draft email, a local file, an unpublished
doc.

## Handoff

Run the gate on the final version. Then report:

- **Ready**: the gate passes. Give the exact final step for the user to take
  (recipients, destination, or delete list) and where the draft is.
- **Blocked**: what prevents readiness, what you tried, and what you need.

List your proof, the reviewer outcome, and remaining risks. State plainly that
nothing was sent, published, or deleted.
