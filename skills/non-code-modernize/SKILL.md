# Non-code Modernize

Modernize the idea, not just the text. Updated dates and numbers do not prove
the work still matters.

## Guardrails

- Work on a copy. Treat the original as the user's.
- Modernizing authorizes updating the copy. It does not authorize sharing,
  sending, deleting the original, or growing its scope.

## Establish intent

Read the work, its comments, and anything it links to. State the smallest
goal it was trying to reach, in one sentence, before judging it.

## Check what changed

- Has the problem already been solved, by the user or someone else?
- Are its facts, numbers, prices, people, tools, or deadlines out of date?
  Check each against a current source you open.
- Does newer work cover the same ground, so this would create two versions
  of the truth?
- Is the original benefit still worth the effort to finish it?

## Choose a disposition

- **MODERNIZE**: still useful, right shape. Update facts, cut stale parts,
  keep the structure.
- **REWORK**: still useful, wrong shape. Describe the smallest rewrite and
  stop unless the user also asked for it.
- **RETIRE**: superseded, obsolete, or not worth it. Cite evidence and stop.
  Do not delete or archive it without permission.

Be willing to recommend RETIRE. Age alone is not evidence, and sunk effort is
not a reason to keep it.

## Report

Lead with MODERNIZE, REWORK, or RETIRE and the reason. Then list what changed
in the world, what you updated with sources, and the remaining decision. Keep
facts separate from judgment, and say what you could not verify.
