# Non-code Takeover

Finish the original goal and keep the original authors' credit. Do not defend
the existing work just because someone already made it.

Read the source: the doc, its comments and edit history, the thread, and the
user's later corrections. Find the current version and any unsaved or
unshared drafts. Treat the handoff as context, not proof.

Work on a copy unless the user asked you to edit in place. Do not overwrite
the original or other people's sections without authorization.

Keep credit. Leave the original authors' names on the work, and note what you
added or changed. Model attribution is not a substitute for their credit.

Use `non-code-modernize` to check the work still fits today's facts and goal.
Rework only what finishing the same goal needs. Cut what no longer serves it,
but do not drop parts the authors clearly intended.

Check facts and links yourself. Say what remains unverified.

If the finish includes sending or publishing, follow `non-code-full-send` or
`non-code-full-stop`, whichever the user asked for; otherwise stop at a ready
draft.

Report what you inherited, what you changed, how you checked it, how credit
was kept, and any remaining decision.
